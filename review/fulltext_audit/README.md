# Full-text reporting audit — 2026-10-30

This is a targeted AI-assisted verification of quantitative reporting in all **111 monitoring papers**. It is separate from the authors' original screening/coding and is not an independent human coding round, replication, or full reclassification of all 185 papers.

## Findings integrated into the EuroS&P manuscript

- **61/111 confirmed quantitative operating reports**, a lower bound. The remaining **50 are unconfirmed**, not classified as proving absence. Twenty-six confirmed positives were N or ID in the earlier abstract-derived field. Eleven previous Y entries are not carried as confirmed positives under this stricter evidence rule; that does not show the original positive was false.
- **15/111 confirmed timing-related evaluations**, another lower bound: 6 advance-warning, 3 detection-delay, 2 temporal-localization, 2 intervention-timing, 1 average-detection-turn, and 1 prefix-early-detection. These are not 15 comparable lead-time measurements.
- **Seven original trace/harness-positive classifications rechecked.** These include natural production observations, recorded task executions, benchmark skill runs, and production-framework test harnesses. They must not be pooled as seven studies of deployed production traffic. Other evaluation-provenance negatives were not exhaustively recoded.
- **MATE retains its real-trace classification:** Section 5.1 (PDF page 8) describes 162 manually collected trajectories from 13 apps and three mobile-agent systems alongside synthetic subsets. An initial synthesis-only hypothesis was rejected after examining the complete evaluation description; no erroneous synthetic-only recoding was applied to the original files.
- **SRM reports later average triggering:** Table 2 (PDF page 8) reports 4.45 versus 4.05 turns despite improved false positives. This is now included in the manuscript so the fraud/state analogy does not imply uniformly earlier detection.

## Evidence contract

`reporting_evidence_ledger.csv` has one row per paper with version, source URL, SHA-256, original field values, audited statuses, one-based PDF-page references, and evidence paraphrases. Page references count the title page, irrespective of printed pagination. No corpus-level negative claim is inferred from an unknown field.

A positive operating-report decision requires inspected numerical false-alarm counts/rates or measured latency, tokens, or inference-call counts. General accuracy/F1 or task-success scores, qualitative efficiency promises, asymptotic complexity, or theoretical budgets alone do not establish this positive. Timing evaluation is typed to distinguish warning from delay, localization, and prefix-only predictions.

Candidates were generated by searching the extracted full texts, then inspected in context, including reported results and appendices. This was a targeted evidence search, not a claim that every sentence of every paper was independently read or every inherited taxonomy label revalidated. Conservative unknowns retain uncertainty where the inspected passages did not establish an affirmative result.

## Reproduction

From the `SoK_v1` directory:

```sh
python3 review/scripts/fulltext_reporting_audit.py
```

The script checks each local PDF against its saved hash, writes the evidence ledger and reporting overlay, and regenerates `summary.json`. Its explicit evidence decisions are inspectable and revisable; it does not infer research conclusions automatically from keywords.

`lit_A_reporting_audited.csv` is a derived overlay. Mechanism, state scope, and evaluation fields remain inherited. Reporting unknowns become `ID`; the original `coding/lit_A_final.csv` is unchanged. Original screening/coding inputs are preserved.

`retrieval_manifest.json` identifies the selected versions and hashes. Later-than-cutoff PDFs were replaced with earlier versions for this audit; later versions remain separately cached. SRM lacks a PDF version header: its version/date was verified against the arXiv submission history and pinned to v1. Retrieval/text preparation utilities are in `review/scripts/`.

The arXiv PDFs and their full-text extracts (`pdf/`, `text/`, `later_versions/`) are not redistributed here. To rebuild them, run `scripts/retrieve_fulltexts.py` and `scripts/pin_fulltext_versions.py`; the audit script then checks each PDF against the SHA-256 recorded in `retrieval_manifest.json`. The ledger, source locators, summaries, and aggregation script are the compact review artifacts.
